The Problem

"On Day 42 of a Gateway mission, Commander Chen wakes with blurred vision — early SANS. Her Flight Surgeon on Earth has a 1.3-second delay but won't see clinical signs for 72 hours. What if the computer already knew?"

Five Threats of Deep-Space Flight

🦴 Bone Loss
❤️ Cardiovascular Deconditioning
👁️ SANS (Neuro-Ocular)
☢️ Radiation Exposure
😴 Sleep Disruption

Our Solution: ASTRAVIVE Architecture

⚙️
Mechanistic ODE Twin
First-principles physiology models for bone density, sleep pressure, SANS risk & radiation dose
🤖
ML Pipeline
Gradient Boosted models with GroupKFold CV, Monte Carlo forecasting & 95% confidence intervals
🔍
XAI Explainability
SHAP-based feature attribution so Flight Surgeons understand every prediction
